# British Voice – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

British Voice turns your speech into a British-accented voice for calls.

- **Your voice stays on your computer.** Speech recognition and voice generation run entirely
  on your own PC. Audio is never uploaded, stored or shared by British Voice.
- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** British Voice collects no personal data.
- **Downloads.** On first use the app downloads free speech and voice model files from
  huggingface.co. Those requests are ordinary file downloads and contain none of your audio.
- **Settings** (your chosen voice and devices) are saved only on your PC, in
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BritishVoice`. Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes everything.
- **Microphone.** The app uses your microphone only while you have pressed **Start**.
